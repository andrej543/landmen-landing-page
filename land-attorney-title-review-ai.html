Review guide

Land attorneys, title review, and AI.

AI can help prepare the file for review, but title conclusions and legal judgment still belong with qualified professionals.

Land attorneys and title attorneys need evidence, context, and clear questions. They do not need a black box that claims to resolve legal issues without review.

What AI can prepare

  • Lease term summaries with source references.
  • Title evidence grouped by tract, owner, date, and instrument.
  • Open questions, missing documents, and possible conflicts for review.
  • Owner and contact context that helps explain the practical path forward.

What attorneys still decide

Ownership conclusions, title opinions, curative strategy, lease interpretation, and legal risk should remain in attorney review. AI can help organize the file and reduce repetitive work; it should not replace legal review.

How Basinfoundry fits

Basinfoundry keeps landman AI close to the evidence. Landmen, attorneys, and VPs of Land can see the same source-backed workflow before work product is delivered.

Questions this page answers

How can AI help land attorneys?

AI can prepare a cleaner review file by grouping instruments, summarizing lease terms, organizing owner context, and surfacing missing documents or conflicts.

What still requires legal judgment?

Title opinions, ownership conclusions, curative strategy, lease interpretation, and legal risk calls should remain with attorneys and qualified reviewers.

What makes AI output review-ready?

Review-ready output is traceable. It includes source references, visible uncertainty, issue status, and a clear path for landmen or attorneys to confirm the work.

Where does Basinfoundry fit in review?

Basinfoundry prepares the shared file before review starts: lease facts, owner context, title evidence, open questions, and project status stay visible in one workflow.
