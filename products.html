Product Details

Open the parts of the system your team cares about most.

Land Management detail

Land Management is the executive operating layer for lease portfolios, title risks, tract maps, new activity, buyout opportunities, and land projects.

  • Best for VP Land, land managers, business development, and executive reporting.
  • Primary outputs: prioritized lease list, risk register, GIS tract view, project board, opportunity screen.
  • Key integrations: county record inputs, GIS layers, owner records, project notes, reporting templates.
Landman Workflows detail

Landman Workflows turns the research path into a controlled system without removing landman judgment from the process.

  • Best for title research, chain of title, owner packets, issue triage, and ROW coordination.
  • Primary outputs: chain-of-title workspace, owner directory, title issue queue, packet history, ROW status.
  • Review advantage: obvious issues are flagged before lawyer review, so review starts from a cleaner file.
Operators detail

Operators gives production and compliance work a repeatable rhythm while keeping land dependencies visible.

  • Best for VP Operations, production reporting, compliance workflows, and executive visibility.
  • Primary outputs: production reporting view, trend dashboard, exception queue, regulatory templates.
  • Operating advantage: land blockers show up next to the operating work they affect.
