How It Works

Structured research without taking the landman out of the work.

Landman Workflows takes the messy parts of title and owner research and turns them into a system: source records stay attached, owner context stays current, title issues are flagged early, and the final file is easier for counsel to review.

01

Title research workspace

Organize source documents, research notes, county record pulls, tract history, and supporting evidence in one file.

02

Chain of title

Build chain paths with source references, gap notes, status, and escalation points instead of burying the story in static files.

03

Mineral and surface owner directory

Maintain owner names, entity context, addresses, phone/email details, contact status, packet history, and ownership notes.

04

Automated title issue flagging

Surface missing documents, gap patterns, owner conflicts, stale contact data, and review questions before the lawyer finds them at the end.

05

ROW workflow support

Track routes, owner contact, consent status, field notes, documents, and handoffs while keeping ROW tied to tract and owner context.

Outputs

What Landman Workflows gives you.

Cleaner review file

Source-backed title paths and issue notes that make review faster and less repetitive.

Owner intelligence

Mineral and surface owner records with contact detail, packets, and follow-up history.

Early issue visibility

Problems show up while the work is moving, not when counsel starts reviewing.

ROW coordination

Route, owner, document, and field status in the same operating record.
