How It Works

A single operating layer for the VP of Land.

Land Management gives leadership the view that usually lives across spreadsheets, maps, inboxes, broker updates, title notes, and status calls. Every tract carries the lease context, title exposure, owner status, operating dependency, project stage, and next action.

01

Lease tracking, analysis, and prioritization

Track lease terms, obligations, expirations, acreage, priorities, and decision status. Rank what needs attention before a deadline becomes a scramble.

02

Title risk register

Surface risk by tract, assign ownership, record evidence, track resolution status, and give counsel a cleaner review path.

03

Coordination with geo and operations

Connect land status to operating plans, development timing, field questions, and geology priorities so the land desk is not operating in a separate universe.

04

Permian activity, BD flags, and buyout screening

Monitor development signals, flag new tract activity, screen buyout opportunities, and show leadership where a land move may be worth pursuing.

05

Project management and GIS tract tracking

Run land projects by tract, stage, owner, file status, and map layer. Keep handoffs visible across landmen, ops, geo, and review.
